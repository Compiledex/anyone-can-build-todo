Simple to-do lists you can share

Make lists, break to-dos into steps, set due dates, and share a list with the people you plan with.

Several lists, shared when you want

Keep home, work and the weekend trip apart. Share a list by username, and its members can add, edit and finish its to-dos.

Break a big to-do into steps

Open a to-do to add steps and tick them off one by one. The row shows how far you are, like 2 of 5 done.

Due dates, repeats, priority and tags

Due dates

Give a to-do a date. Once the day has passed, it says Overdue in red.

Repeats

Daily, weekly or monthly. Mark it done, and the next one is ready with its new date.

Priority

Mark a to-do Low, Medium or High, so the important ones stand out.

Tags and notes

Tag a to-do with words like work or home, and keep a few notes with it.

Find a to-do, or put them in your order

Search, filter and sort

Search titles, notes and tags. Show all, not done or done. Sort by date, priority, title or your own order.

Your own order

Drag a to-do by its handle, or use the arrow buttons to move it up or down.

The same order for everyone

The order belongs to the list, so the people you share it with see it too.

Only you and the people you share a list with can see it.

Every list needs a login. Someone you have not shared a list with cannot open it, or even tell that it exists.

Light or dark, like your system

The app follows the light or dark setting of your computer or phone. Both are easy to read.

Start with one list
